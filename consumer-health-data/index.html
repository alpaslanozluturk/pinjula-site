Pinjula — Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
Contact: support@pinjula.com

For: residents of Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, and anyone whose consumer health data is collected there. We apply it to everyone in the United States and Canada. It covers one thing: the photograph of a receipt that you choose to scan in Pinjula.

1. What consumer health data we collect, and why

What. Only what a supermarket receipt you choose to scan may show about your health: for example a purchase of medicine, including prescribed medication, or another health-related purchase. Allergies, intolerances, diets and the other things you enter about the people you cook for stay on your phone and are not collected by us.

Why, and how it is used. We use the photograph only to read the food and drink lines on the receipt and send them back to your phone, so that you can add them to your pantry. We use it for nothing else. We do not store the photograph. Google, which reads it for us under a written agreement, does not use it to train its models and keeps the request for up to 55 days only to detect abuse.

Your consent. The app asks for your consent before the first scan, and says what the photograph is, what it is used for, that it goes to Google as our service provider, and how to withdraw. We collect it only after you agree, and we ask again if the words of the question change in what they say.

2. Where it comes from

From you, when you take the photograph or choose it from your photos.

3. Who receives it

No third party. Google (the Gemini API), which reads the photograph, and Cloudflare, which runs our network, process it only on our behalf, under written agreements that bind them to protect it and to use it only for us. We share it in no other way.

4. Third parties and affiliates

We share consumer health data with no third party and have no affiliates.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we have collected or shared your consumer health data, and to give you access to it, including a list of every third party and affiliate it was shared with (there are none); to withdraw your consent; and to delete it, and to tell our service providers to delete it.

  • Withdraw your consent in the app: Settings → Privacy & security → Receipt scanning. The app asks again before the next scan.
  • For anything else, write to support@pinjula.com. We verify your request by replying to the address you write from.
  • We answer within 45 days. We may extend once by 45 days, and we tell you why. Requests are free.

Google's abuse-monitoring copy of a request cannot be matched to a person, so we cannot find it; it expires within 55 days.

6. If we refuse: appeal

If we refuse a request, reply to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We decide within 45 days and explain our decision. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to your state attorney general: Washington (https://www.atg.wa.gov), Connecticut (https://portal.ct.gov/ag) or Nevada (https://ag.nv.gov).

7. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not geofence

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not use a geofence around a health-care facility to collect it or to send you a message.

8. Nevada

  • To review your information, or to ask us to change it, write to support@pinjula.com.
  • Changes to this page are announced here, and in the app, before they take effect.
  • No third party collects consumer health data about you across websites or apps through Pinjula.
  • Effective date: 5 October 2026.

9. Changes

When we change this page we change the effective date above. We ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
