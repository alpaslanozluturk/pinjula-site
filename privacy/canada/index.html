Privacy Notice — Canada and Québec supplement — Pinjula

Effective date: 11 October 2026
Contact: support@pinjula.com

This page adds to the Privacy Notice and applies if you live in Canada. Where it gives you more than the notice, it applies to you. It applies together with the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Alberta's and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Acts and Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector. The French version follows once the English text has been reviewed.

Who is responsible

The publisher of Pinjula, Alpaslan Özlütürk, is accountable for the personal information we hold. The publisher is also the person in charge of the protection of personal information (Responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels, Québec). Title: publisher, person with the highest authority. Contact: support@pinjula.com. This person answers questions about our service providers outside Canada.

Consent

We rely on your consent. We ask for it separately for each purpose and never fold it into accepting the Terms of Use: for usage statistics, and for the AI features. You can withdraw any consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & security; the consequence of withdrawing is that the related feature stops working. We use your information only for the purposes in the notice.

Privacy by default (Québec and everywhere in Canada)

Usage statistics and the AI features are off until you turn them on, and nothing is sent for them before. The app knows only your country, not your province, so this is how it works in all of Canada. Your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it are part of every connection to our server; Cloudflare keeps them in its record of each request for up to seven days, and we use them only for faults and attacks, not to locate you.

Where your information goes

Your account is stored in Frankfurt, Germany, and processed by our service providers in the United States, the European Union, Singapore and wherever Google or its sub-processors operate — Supabase (sign-in), Cloudflare (network), RevenueCat (subscriptions), PostHog (usage statistics, only if you allow them), Google (AI features, only if you use them) and Resend (emails) — and by us in Türkiye. Your information may therefore be communicated outside Québec and Canada and may be accessible to the courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities of those countries. Before using these providers we assess the risks, taking into account the sensitivity of the information, the purposes, the safeguards and the legal regime of the destination, and we record the assessment in writing. To get written information about our practices with service providers outside Canada, or to ask about them, write to the person in charge above (Alberta Personal Information Protection Act, s. 13.1).

How we govern personal information

The publisher of Pinjula decides what we collect and is the only person at Pinjula with access to it. We keep each kind of information only as long as stated in the notice under "How long we keep things" and then delete it. We keep a register of every confidentiality incident for five years, and a record of every breach of security safeguards for 24 months. If an incident creates a risk of serious injury (Québec) or a real risk of significant harm (federal, Alberta), we notify the people affected and the Commission d'accès à l'information, the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Alberta Commissioner, as the law requires. To complain, write to the person in charge above; we reply within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to withdraw consent, to delete your account, and to receive a copy of your information in a structured, commonly used format (portability). There are two ways to ask: in the app (Settings → Account: "Export my data", "Delete account", and the switches under Settings → Privacy & security) or by email to the person in charge at support@pinjula.com. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (https://www.priv.gc.ca); in Québec, to the Commission d'accès à l'information (https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca); in Alberta, to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta (https://oipc.ab.ca); in British Columbia, to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (https://www.oipc.bc.ca).

Automated processing

The AI features generate recipes, menus and cooking plans when you ask. We make no decision about you based exclusively on automated processing of your personal information.

Subscription notices

The email notices described in the notice under "Premium, payments and subscription emails" include, for Canada, a notice before a free trial ends and, after a purchase, a link to our Terms of Use and our seller details. They are sent in French first, then English, to meet a legal duty and to answer your purchase; they are not marketing.

Language

In Québec, the French version of the notice, of this page and of our Terms is the version you accept unless, after receiving it, you expressly choose the English version.
