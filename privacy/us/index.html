Privacy Notice — United States supplement — Pinjula

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Contact: support@pinjula.com

This page adds to the Privacy Notice and applies if you live in the United States. Where it gives you more than the notice, it applies to you. We apply it to every US resident, whether or not a particular state's law requires it. It is written to meet California's and Delaware's online privacy notice laws, Nevada's online notice law and the notice duties of the Connecticut, Texas and Nebraska data privacy laws.

What we collect, and from where

  • Identifiers: if you sign in, the email address and name your Google or Apple account shares (held by our sign-in service, Supabase), an internal account number, and your IP address (seen by every server you reach); and — only if you allow usage statistics — a random ID for your phone. Sources: you, Google or Apple, and your phone. Purposes: running your account, security, and usage statistics.
  • Commercial information: if you subscribe, your subscription status and purchase records (product, price, dates, whether you are in a trial or have cancelled), received from the App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. Purposes: confirming Premium, sending the subscription notices, and counting trials, renewals and cancellations.
  • Internet or app activity: usage-statistics events, only if you allow them; Cloudflare's record of each request, kept up to seven days; and, if you use the AI features, how many AI requests your account sent each month, kept under a code derived from your account until the end of the following month, to hold Premium to its monthly limit.
  • Approximate location: the country, region, city, postal code and coordinates Cloudflare works out from your IP address, kept up to seven days in its request record. The app never asks for your location.
  • Information you give us: dish names you type for the AI recipe feature (and, with the AI features, the number of people you cook for), and reports about a recipe (stored without any identifier).
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect the allergies, intolerances, diets or other health information you enter; it stays on your phone. The catalogue dishes sent for a menu may reflect an allergy or a religious belief; they are sent only after your explicit consent, to give you the feature you asked for.

We do not collect precise location, government IDs, biometric data, payment card details (the store takes payment), your contacts or photo library, or advertising identifiers.

Who we disclose it to

Service providers acting on our behalf and instructions: Cloudflare (network and storage), Supabase (sign-in and database), RevenueCat (subscriptions), PostHog (usage statistics, if you allow them), Google (the AI features, when you use one) and Resend (subscription emails). Porkbun, which hosts our support mailbox, and GitHub Pages, which hosts our website (a visitor's IP address). Apple and Google as app stores and sign-in providers, and Apple when our server asks it to revoke the app's sign-in tokens after you delete your account. Authorities, where the law requires it. A successor, if the service is transferred. We disclose personal information to no other third party. The full list, with where each processes information, is in the notice under "Who receives it, and where".

We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell it, we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising or targeted advertising, and the app has no advertising library. We do not use personal information to profile you or to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. We do not use personal information to train large language models, and Google does not use what we send to its Gemini API to train its models. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 18.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Pinjula does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and no other party collects information about your activity over time and across other services through Pinjula, with one exception: on iPhone the app contains Google's sign-in library, which collects under Google's own privacy policy (we declare what it collects in the App Store and Google Play forms). Because there is no such tracking, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. Usage statistics work only inside Pinjula, are off until you allow them, and can be turned off in Settings. Nevada residents may send a request not to sell their covered information to support@pinjula.com. We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we process your personal information and to give you a copy; to correct it; to delete it; to give you a portable copy; and to stop any processing that relies on your consent. You can also ask for the list of third parties we have disclosed it to; we have disclosed it to none other than the service providers named above.

There are two ways to ask. In the app, use Settings → Account → Export my data or Delete account, and the switches under Settings → Privacy & security. Or write to support@pinjula.com from the email address you sign in with — we verify a request by replying to that address — or, if you have no account, tell us enough to find what you ask about. We answer within 45 days and may extend once by 45 days, telling you why. Requests are free, and we do not treat you differently for making one. Someone you authorise in writing may make a request for you.

If we refuse, you can appeal by replying with "Appeal" in the subject line. We decide within 45 days, explain the decision and tell you how to contact your state attorney general: Washington (https://www.atg.wa.gov), Connecticut (https://portal.ct.gov/ag), Texas (https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov), Nebraska (https://ago.nebraska.gov), Nevada (https://ag.nv.gov), or the attorney general of your own state.

Consumer health data

We do not collect consumer health data. Our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which applies to Washington, Nevada and Connecticut residents and which we apply to everyone in the United States, says so and how to ask us about it.

Children

Pinjula is for adults and is not directed to children under 13 or under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Subscriptions

The notices we send for yearly plans and the annual reminder are described in the notice under "Premium, payments and subscription emails". The subscription terms are in the Terms of Use.

Security incidents

If personal information is breached, we notify you and the authorities as the law requires — under state breach laws and, where it applies, the Federal Trade Commission's Health Breach Notification Rule. Our server holds no Social Security numbers, no payment card data and no passwords, and none of your health information.

Language

Connecticut's law asks for the privacy notice in each language a product is offered in. See the notice under "Languages".
