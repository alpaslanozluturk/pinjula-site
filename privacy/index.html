Summary

This is the short version of our Privacy Notice; the full notice applies if the two differ. Each line says what we collect, where it goes, why, and how long it is kept.

  • Stays on your phone. What: the people you cook for and what you enter about them (names, age bands, portion sizes, allergies with severity, intolerances, diets, disliked ingredients), your pantry, shopping list, calendar, cookbook, ratings and notes, and settings. Goes to: nobody. We never receive your allergies, diets, dislikes, household or ratings; the only items that can leave are a dish name you type and the number of people you cook for (inside an AI request), the app's language (with AI requests and reports) and a report you choose to send. Why: so the app can filter and plan on your phone. Kept: until you delete it, delete your account or uninstall. Your phone's own backup to your Google or Apple account may include it.
  • Dish pictures. What: the app asks our server for the pictures of the dishes it shows; the request names the dishes, which were chosen with your household's filters, so the list can indirectly reflect what your household avoids, and it shows your IP address. Goes to: our server, on Cloudflare. Why: the pictures are not inside the app. Kept: Cloudflare's request record, up to 7 days, used only for faults and attacks.
  • Signing in (only if you sign in). What: your email address, name, profile-picture address and account ID from Google or Apple; your sessions. Goes to: our sign-in service (Supabase, Frankfurt, Germany). Why: to run your account, which Premium and the AI features need. Kept: until you delete your account (sessions: until you sign out or they expire); Supabase's own logs of sign-in requests and events, under Supabase's retention (see "How long we keep things").
  • Premium (only if you subscribe). What: your purchase records from the store, under your account ID, and your email address for the notices we must send. Goes to: RevenueCat and Resend (both United States) and our database. The store takes the payment; we never see your card. Why: to confirm Premium, send subscription notices and show that you agreed to the subscription. Kept: our records while active and 12 months after; a minimal record of each charge, with no name, email address or account ID, three years, even after you delete your account; RevenueCat until you delete your account; Resend under its own terms.
  • AI features (only after you allow them). What: the dish names and details the feature needs, or a dish name you typed — never your allergies, diets, pantry or who is in your household (only how many people you cook for), though the list of dishes chosen for a menu can reflect what your household avoids. Goes to: our server, then Google's Gemini. Why: to mix cuisines in one menu, plan how to cook a menu, or write a recipe for a dish you typed. Kept: Google, up to 55 days; we keep a count of dish names we had no recipe for, and how many AI requests your account sent each month, for Premium's monthly limit (until the end of the following month).
  • Reports (only when you send one). What: a reason, your note, and a recipe's code or, for a recipe the app wrote, its text. Goes to: our database in Frankfurt. Why: to correct recipes. Kept: 12 months, with nothing that says who sent it.
  • Usage statistics (off until you say yes). What: counts of screens and features used, under a random ID for the phone; never anything about your allergies, diets or household. Goes to: PostHog's EU cloud, Frankfurt. Why: to see what to improve. Kept: one year.
  • Emails you send us. Goes to: our mailbox, hosted by Porkbun. Kept: 12 months after the last message.

No advertising, no sale of data. The app has no advertising library and no crash-reporting library, and the statistics library's own error reporting is switched off. (If your phone shares diagnostics with developers, Apple or Google may give us crash reports under their own policies.) We do not sell your information or share it for targeted advertising. Adults only: Pinjula is for people 18 and older; if your app store tells the app you are under 18, the app goes no further. You decide: the free version works without an account, statistics and the AI features each ask first, and you can export your data and delete your account in the app.
