Not medical advice; allergens and diets

Pinjula is a cooking and meal-planning tool. It does not give medical, nutritional or dietary advice and does not replace a doctor, allergist or dietitian. The allergen, intolerance and diet filters compare recipes in our catalogue with the information you enter on your phone. They can be wrong or incomplete: an ingredient can be missing or misclassified, products and brands differ, and foods can contain allergens through cross-contact. A recipe that passes a filter is not certified as safe or free from any allergen, and halal or kosher filters only exclude obvious conflicts. Always read the full ingredient list and the label of every product you use, and do not rely on Pinjula where a mistake could cause serious harm. Nutrition figures are estimates calculated from food-composition databases, not an analysis of the food you cook. Pinjula is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

AI features

Some Premium features use Google's Gemini model to write a recipe for a dish you name, or to compose or describe a menu. AI output is produced automatically, is not reviewed by us, may be inaccurate, incomplete or unsafe, and never includes an allergen, diet or nutrition check. A recipe written for a dish you typed carries no allergen, diet or nutrition information at all. Check every ingredient against your own and your household's allergies, follow safe food-handling practice and cooking temperatures, and use your own judgement. Do not use the AI features for medical purposes. You can report a problem with any recipe in the app.

The AI features run only if you allow them. When you reach for one and have not allowed them yet, Pinjula asks on a screen of its own — before the Premium screen and before any sign-in — and the permission is separate from your subscription: you can withdraw it at any time in Settings, and everything else in Premium works without it. Declining or withdrawing the permission does not by itself entitle you to a refund; refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules (see "Refunds" above).

Fair use. Premium includes up to 150 requests to the AI features each calendar month, counted for your account in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), the three AI features together. A request is one message the app sends to the AI service, and some actions send more than one: a week plan that mixes cuisines sends at most one for each day, and the app may ask once more when too few of the menus that come back suit your request. A request counts as soon as it is sent, unless the AI service gives no answer: if the AI service cannot be reached or reports an error, the request is given back. An answer that cannot be used and an answer that arrives after the app has stopped waiting still count. When this month's requests are used, the AI features pause until 00:00 UTC on the first day of the next month (in the United States and Canada, the afternoon or evening before, local time) — the app tells you the date — and everything else in Premium keeps working. The limit is there so that one account cannot run up costs the subscription price does not cover; we change it only as described in "Changes to these terms".
