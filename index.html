Dinner ideas for everyone at your table

Pinjula suggests home-cooked recipes and full meals from sixteen cuisines, filtered for the allergies, diets and dislikes of the people you cook for.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Sixteen cuisines, cooked at home

From Turkey to Korea and Mexico to Iran: recipes and whole meals people actually cook in their own kitchens, not restaurant showpieces.

Everyone at the table

Allergies, diets and dislikes for each person you cook for. Recipes containing a listed allergen are filtered out.

Plan the week

A calendar, a shopping list that knows your pantry, and reminders for leftovers.

No repeats

A dish you cooked does not come back for seven days, so the week stays varied.

The recipes, cultural notes and translations were written in-house with AI assistance and have not been reviewed by cooks from each culture; tell us when something is wrong. Allergen filtering is an aid, not a guarantee: always check the full ingredient list yourself. Pinjula is not a medical device and nutrition figures are estimates.
