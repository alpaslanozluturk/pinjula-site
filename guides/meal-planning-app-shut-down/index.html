What do you do when your meal planning app shuts down?

Save what you can while the app still opens: the recipes you cook most, the household list (who eats what) and the staples you always buy. If the app is already gone, rebuild from memory in an afternoon: your ten or fifteen regular dinners, each person's restrictions, and your usual shopping staples. Then choose a replacement that keeps your data where you can reach it.

First, keep what you can reach

If your old app still opens, or you have it open on a device:

  • Copy the text of your favorite recipes into a notes app or a document. Include quantities.
  • Screenshot meal plans and shopping lists you want to reuse.
  • Check your email for a closing notice; it sometimes names a date and what will happen to your data.
  • Do not assume an export exists. Some services delete personal data on closing and offer no export.

If the app has already closed and your data is gone, do not spend days trying to recover it. Most households cook from a small set of dishes you can rebuild in a short list.

Rebuild in four lists

  1. Your regular dinners. Write the 10 to 15 dishes you actually cook. Do not add aspirational ones.
  2. The people at the table. For each: allergies (as described by their doctor), diets, lactose avoidance, dislikes.
  3. Your kitchen. Appliances you own and ones you do not.
  4. Your staples. What you always keep in the cupboard, so a shopping list only holds what is missing.

This is the data every planner, paper or digital, needs from you. Having it written once makes switching again later easy.

Choose what to rely on

Some questions worth asking of any new planner, whatever it is:

  • Where is my data stored, and can I get it out?
  • Can I use it without creating an account?
  • What happens to my data if the service closes?
  • Does it plan for everyone at my table, or for one person?
  • What is free, and what costs money? Read the store listing for the price before you subscribe.

Plain paper and a notes app also work, and cost nothing. Pick whatever you will open on a Sunday evening.

How Pinjula helps

Pinjula is a meal-planning app for iPhone, iPad and Android, sold in the United States and Canada. Everything in the free plan works without an account. The people you cook for, what they declared, your ratings, your pantry and your plans stay on your phone; you sign in with Apple or Google only to start Premium. You add each person at the table (allergies, diets, lactose avoidance, dislikes), set your kitchen equipment, and the app suggests recipes and full meals from sixteen cuisines that fit the whole table. Recipes containing the allergens you list are filtered out. Always check the full ingredient list yourself. Pinjula is not a medical device. The recipes, cultural notes and translations were written in-house with AI assistance and have not been reviewed by cooks from the cultures they come from.

Honest limits

Pinjula starts from an empty profile and its own catalog of recipes. We do not claim it can import plans or recipes from another service. If your old recipes were your own, keep copies in a notes app, because no planner can promise to hold them forever. Because what you enter stays on your phone, keep your phone's own backup to your Google or Apple account switched on: it may include the app's data, and a phone lost without a backup means starting your lists again.

FAQ

Can I get my data back from a closed meal planning app?

Maybe, if the service sent a notice or still lets you in. Check your email and the app itself, and copy out anything you want to keep as soon as you can. Many services delete data when they close and offer no export.

How do I start meal planning again after losing my plans?

List 10 to 15 dinners you actually cook, who eats what, the equipment you have and your staples. That is enough to start.

Do I need an account for a meal planner?

Not always. Pinjula's free plan works without signing in, and what you enter stays on your phone.

How far ahead can I plan in Pinjula?

Up to three days on the free plan, and the whole week with Premium.

Where does Pinjula keep my household details?

On your phone. Usage statistics are sent only if you switch them on.
