Pinjula in one sentence

Pinjula suggests home recipes and full meals from sixteen cuisines, filtered for the allergies, diets and dislikes of everyone you cook for.

About Pinjula

Cooking for a household often means remembering a different list for every person: one avoids milk, one is vegetarian, one has a nut allergy. Pinjula keeps those lists for each person and suggests dinners from sixteen cuisines that leave out the allergens listed for anyone at the table. It also takes the kitchen into account: the equipment you own and, if you like, only what is already in your pantry. A calendar, a cookbook, a pantry and a shopping list come with it.

The people you cook for and what they declared stay on the phone; the free plan works without an account. Premium, a subscription, adds planning the whole week, nutrition estimates and goals, the keto and high-protein filters, optional AI help and home-screen widgets. Allergy filters are never part of Premium.

Facts

  • Launch: November 2026, in the United States and Canada.
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 or later), Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 or later).
  • Catalogue: 2,240 home recipes from sixteen cuisines: Turkish, Greek, Lebanese, Italian, French, Spanish, British, Japanese, Indian, Thai, Korean, Mexican, American, German, Chinese and Iranian.
  • Languages: English, French, Spanish, German, Italian and Turkish.
  • Price: a free plan; Premium at US$4.99 a month or US$34.99 a year in the United States and CA$6.49 a month in Canada, with a one-week free trial for those eligible.
  • Made by: an independent developer.

Good to know

  • The recipes, cultural notes and translations were written in-house with AI assistance and have not been reviewed by cooks from the cultures they come from. Dish and ingredient pictures are AI-generated.
  • Recipes containing the allergens you list are filtered out; always check the full ingredient list yourself. Allergen filtering is an aid, not a guarantee.
  • Pinjula is not a medical device, and nutrition figures are estimates, not advice.
